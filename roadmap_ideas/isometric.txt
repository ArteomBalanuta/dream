# Design Specification: Dark Gothic Isometric Visual Architecture

## 1. Core Technical Objective

Replicate the visual weight, organic grime, and high-contrast atmospheric depth of early-2000s dark-fantasy isometric rendering (e.g., *MU Online*, *Diablo II*), avoiding the clean, vector-like, or overly smooth presentation of modern casual titles.

---

## 2. Rendering Pipelines & Lighting Constraints

### 2.1 Color Grading & Dynamic Range

* **Tone Mapping:** Standard LDR (Low Dynamic Range) with intentional shadow crushing. Avoid wide HDR tone-mapping that lifts black levels.
* **Palette Baseline:** Earthy, desaturated tones—decayed moss green, damp stone gray, weathered oak brown, oxidized iron.
* **Saturation Rules:** Base environment saturation capped at **25–35%**. High-value magical effects, glowing armor, or drops are permitted high saturation (**80–100%**) to create contrast against the dark background.

### 2.2 Lighting & Shadow Mechanics

* **Light Attenuation:** Steep fall-off curves for point lights. No global ambient lighting above **15% luminosity**.
* **Baked Ambient Occlusion (AO):** High-density AO baked directly into diffuse textures at object intersections (wall bases, barrel undersides, ground contacts).
* **Hard Shadow Casts:** Directional sun/moon shadow angles fixed at **45°–60°** with minimal soft-shadow filtering (crisp, defined shadow edges).

---

## 3. Texture & Surface Pipeline

### 3.1 Micro-Detail & Noise Rules

* **Photo-Sampled Texturing:** Textures must incorporate real-world surface imperfections (grime, water stains, surface pitting, fractured stone, lichen growth).
* **Procedural Avoidance:** Strictly prohibit flat color fills or smooth vector gradients on terrain and structural assets.
* **Texture Contrast:** High localized micro-contrast on diffuse maps to ensure texture details remain legible under zoomed-out isometric cameras.

### 3.2 Material Properties (Non-PBR / Stylized-PBR Hybrid)

* **Roughness Floor:** Minimum roughness parameter of **0.65** across environment assets (prevents uniform modern plastic sheen).
* **Specular Maps:** Metallic highlights restricted to hand-painted specular maps (concentrated on armor plates, weapon edges, wet cobblestone tops).

---

## 4. Geometry & Silhouette Guidelines

### 4.1 Structural Mesh Constraints

* **Low-to-Mid Poly Silhouette:** Maintain sharp angular edges on architectural assets (roof tiles, fence posts, stone arches). Avoid excessive subdividing or edge-smoothing.
* **Density Clusters:** Break clean geometric lines with scattered secondary props (broken barrels, detached wagon wheels, fallen armor fragments, stray stone debris).

### 4.2 Foliage & Transparency

* **Alpha-Tested Cards:** Trees, bushes, and palm leaves must use hard alpha cutout cards (clip threshold: `0.5`) rather than soft alpha blending or modern sub-surface scattering.
* **Internal Leaf Contrast:** Leaf textures require heavy internal dark shading between fronds to create visual depth and physical weight.

---

## 5. Camera & Spatial Framing

* **Projection:** Orthographic or low-FOV Perspective (FOV **15°–25°** max).
* **Pitch Angle:** Fixed isometric inclination (**45°–55°** relative to ground plane).
* **Scale Compression:** High asset spatial density—compress ground distance between interactive points of interest to maximize visual information within the viewport.

---

## 6. UI & HUD Aesthetics (Skeuomorphic Paradigm)

* **Physical Frame Integration:** The HUD must sit inside carved, heavy-metal or stone frames featuring dark fantasy motifs (gargoyles, dragon scale relief, rusted rivets).
* **Tactile Gauge Displays:** Fluid/resource indicators must use classic glass orb or heavy-gauge structures with liquid refraction overlays.
* **No Translucent Vector UI:** Ban flat, borderless, or minimalist neon UI components. UI elements must feel like physical artifacts within the universe.

---

## Agent Prompt / Context Template

> **Agent Directive:** When generating visual shaders, texturing assets, UI designs, or camera layouts for this module, enforce:
> 1. *Desaturated dirty textures with baked-in contact shadows.*
> 2. *Crushed black values with high-contrast localized lighting.*
> 3. *Hard-edged angular geometry with high spatial prop density.*
> 4. *Skeuomorphic metal/stone UI frames.*
> Reject modern soft-light, vibrant flat-shading, or vector-minimalist paradigms.
