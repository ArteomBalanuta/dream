# Cleave: a timed swing

- **Purpose**: turn the Warrior's `skill.cleave` (kind `MeleeArc`) from an instant hit into a readable, timed swing.
- **Status**: implemented on `cleave/int`.
- **Scope**: content (`skills.json`), the authoritative melee resolution, the player animation clip and state
  machine, the arc slash effect, and a quickbar icon. No protocol bump (stays 22), no persistence change.

## Overview

Cleave costs 5 mana and hits every valid target in its frontal cone. One shared tick timeline - wind-up, active,
recovery - is defined once in code. The server deals damage only inside the active window, at most once per target
per cast. A new `cleave` clip on the player model sweeps the weapon left to right in step with that window. The
slash effect appears as the swing turns active, and its outer edge lands exactly on the hit range. Cleave gets a
real quickbar icon instead of the "Cl" letters.

## Design decisions

1. **One timeline.** `dream/Simulation/Combat/MeleeArcSwing.cs` holds `WindupTicks` 20, `ActiveTicks` 10,
   `RecoveryTicks` 24 and the derived `TotalTicks` and seconds (at 64 ticks/s). The server, the animation state
   machine, the effect and the clip test all read it. The Python clip generator repeats the tick numbers (it may not
   read other files); a C# test pins the shipped clip keyframes to the C# constants.
2. **Server-delayed hit.** An accepted MeleeArc cast spends mana, starts the cooldown, ends spawn protection,
   queues the `CastEventDelta` as before and returns `AbilityUsedResult(key, 0, 0, 0)`; it then stores a pending
   swing. The swing resolves the whole cone every tick in `[cast + WindupTicks, cast + WindupTicks + ActiveTicks)`
   from the caster's current position, with the direction fixed at cast. A per-cast hit set makes each target take
   at most one hit. Every hit tick re-checks that the session still resolves to the same combatant, that the caster
   is spawned and alive and in Combat mode; otherwise the swing is cancelled with no refund. Pending swings run in
   cast order, are pooled (steady state allocates nothing) and are never persisted. `ProcessSkillUse` stays instant
   for direct callers.
3. **All targets.** Omitting `maxTargets` on a MeleeArc skill means no cap (`SkillDefinition.AllTargets`). An
   explicit value is still validated 1..16, and the cap path stays, tested with a synthetic capped skill.
4. **Animation on acceptance.** The local player's accepted `AbilityUsedResult` for a MeleeArc skill starts the
   clip. For a remote player, a MeleeArc `CastEventDelta` in a snapshot starts it.
5. **Effect from the same numbers.** The crescent atlas cell is repainted with exact geometry (centred, outer edge at
   a known radius, 120 degrees, dim at the caster's left tip and bright at the right). The quad half-size is
   `range / OuterRadiusFraction`. The effect waits `WindupSeconds`, then sweeps sparks along the edge left to right
   over `ActiveSeconds`.
6. **Icon.** A 1x1 `skill.cleave` sprite in the items atlas; the quickbar already resolves sprites by `iconKey`.

## Contracts

- `MeleeArcSwing`: the timeline above, every seconds value derived from the tick counts.
- Trailing optional parameter `int SkillSwingSerial = 0` on `PlayerAnimationInputs`, `LocalPlayerFrameInputs` and
  `RemotePlayerPresentationState`. A changed serial starts the cleave clip.
- `PlayerAnimationStateMachine.CleaveClip = "cleave"`, `CleaveSeconds = MeleeArcSwing.TotalSeconds`. Cleave
  interrupts a melee swing; an attack serial that changes while it plays is consumed and ignored.
- `AuthoritativeCombatResolver.ResolveMeleeArcSweep(...)` with a per-cast `HashSet<long>` keyed on kind and actor id.
- `FxArcGeometry`: `PaintedArcDegrees` 120, `OuterRadiusFraction` 24/32, `HalfSizeForRange(range)`.
- Unchanged on purpose: the sprite name `skill.cleave`, `CastEventDelta`, `AbilityUsedResult`, `SkillCastEvent`,
  protocol 22.

## Acceptance

- Cleave costs 5 mana; at 4 mana it is refused with mana and cooldown unchanged; at 5 it is accepted and mana drops
  by 5.
- 20 enemies in the arc, in range, with clear line of sight: all 20 are hit exactly once, none before
  `cast + WindupTicks` and none at or after `cast + WindupTicks + ActiveTicks`.
- An enemy in the arc for the whole window takes one damage event; one that walks in during the window is hit; one
  that leaves before the window is not.
- If the caster dies, leaves or switches to Building during the wind-up, nothing is hit.
- PvP is unchanged in kind, and lockstep determinism still holds.
- `player-grim.glb` has a `cleave` clip with keys at the timeline's times and length `MeleeArcSwing.TotalSeconds`;
  the weapon tip moves left, then forward, then right.
- The state machine plays cleave for `CleaveSeconds` on a new skill serial; a snapshot cast event for a remote
  player increments that player's serial; the local player's own accepted cast does the same.
- An arc cast spawns nothing before `WindupSeconds`, then one crescent quad of half-size
  `range / OuterRadiusFraction`, with sparks moving left to right.
- The painted crescent matches `FxArcGeometry` within 1 px of radius and 6 degrees of angle, and every shipped
  MeleeArc skill's `ArcDegrees` equals `PaintedArcDegrees`.
- The quickbar draws the `skill.cleave` sprite, not letters.

## Out of scope

A skill book panel (the hotbar is the skill menu), tooltips, a client refusal notice, Bolt and Mend icons, cursor
aim, client-side prediction of the swing, and animating other skill kinds.
