# Work log: LDR effects wave and enhancement round

Branch `integrate/fx-ldr`, from master 7264b34. Plans: `docs/ledger/plans/2026-11-02-fx-ldr-plan.md` and `2026-11-02-fx-ldr-enhancement-plan.md`.

## Scope

Effects in LDR on the back buffer. HDR, bloom, tone mapping and FXAA were left out on purpose: none exist in the engine, `roadmap_ideas/isometric.txt` asks for LDR with crushed blacks, and gamma-space additive blending is the classic look. Aura shells and weapon trails were deferred because the character shows no gear or weapon.

## What landed

- One instanced premultiplied effects pass after the sky: pool of 1,888 particles, 128 halo and flame slots, 32 ground shadows.
- Hit sparks stretched along velocity, kill bursts with ash and a flash sprite, torch and lamp ember plumes, flame sprites, light-catching dust, soft halos.
- Transient flash lights that never displace a collected light.
- Contact blobs under players and enemies, with a ground probe that handles subdivided cells and ledges.
- Emissive texture sampling with UV scroll in the world shaders, gated by a flag. No asset uses it yet.
- `dreamFogTransmittance`, an `fx` GPU timer section, a client-side `ActorDiedEvent`, and `StartFocused` off during HUD capture.
- Procedural atlas `dream/ui/particles/particles.png` from `tools/ui-assets/gen_particles.py`.
- A missing atlas or failed fx shader gives one `effects` fault line and a game without effects.

## Parked

- **Iso sizes.** Embers are about 1 px at 18 m and spark width and the kill burst weren't seen in iso, because `fight:` doesn't start a fight there. Tune `FxSimulation` constants on the merged view, using click-to-move in the script.
- **Iso noon flame.** With no torch model it reads as a cone floating beside the character.
- **Blob.** It grounds the feet but stays subtle. Ledge and rubble behaviour is unit tested only; no capture reached it.
- **Lamps.** Halo and plume are unit tested only; the capture script can't place a lamp.
- **Emissive.** Compiles and leaves non-emissive materials unchanged; no content exercises it.
- **Remote-player fire.** Depends on `HeldLight`, None until protocol v6.
- **First-person near-camera cull** is global, so a biting wolf loses its blob. First person goes away with iso.
- **Point light height.** The light stays 1.8 m above the feet, so the ground pool doesn't match the flame height.
- **No real shader compile in tests.** The repo has no GL test path, so GLSL is checked only by running the game.
